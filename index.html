    First-person chemistry sandbox · early build

    Reaction
    Lab

    Pick things up. Pour them together. Find out.

    Helpers
    Move
    W A S D · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch · Q / E lean
    Hands
    Hold left mouse to grab · let go to drop, swing to throw · wheel pushes and pulls · R + mouse rotates · X sets it down gently
    Pour / use
    Hold right mouse while holding a container to pour, or to use the tool in your hand
    Tools
    1 tongs · 2 spatula · 3 pipette · 4 thermometer · 5 extinguisher · Story Mode: 6 rock pick (break off samples, crush lumps to powder) · 7 trowel (dig sand) · press again to put away
    Story
    F opens doors, sleeps in your bed and fills a held container at a tap, the stream or the lake · H goals · the Materials tab in the book lists what you have found
    Other
    F use a switch or tap, or the torch · E inspect · Tab Discovery Book · P periodic table · N notebook · Esc menu · Z fast-forward time (×4, ×16) · H experiment guide (Student mode)
    Touch
    Left thumb walks, drag to look, tap a thing to pick it up and tap again to let go

    Physics of grabbing and carrying, containers and pouring, and a rules-based chemistry engine with 41 reactions, including acids, bases, indicators, crystals, smelting and glassmaking. All chemistry is simplified for play. It is not a guide to real procedures.
