## What it is

- A co-operative multiplayer physics game in which a small team grabs and carries objects in a dark, dangerous facility.
- Built around physics: almost everything is a movable object, and players can pick up, throw and stack most of them.
- Players work together to collect valuables and return them, while threats hunt the team.

1. A run starts and the team enters the facility.
2. Players locate valuable objects and move them toward the exit or extraction point.
3. Collected value counts toward a goal or quota **(unsure: the exact quota rules)**.
